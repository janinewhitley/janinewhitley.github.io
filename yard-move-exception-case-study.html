Yard Move Exception Workflow

Hero

What Is It?

An internal workflow process improvement that allows yard drivers resolve an incorrect yard state without abandoning their active task.

Role

UX Designer

Scope

Research, workflow design, interaction design, prototyping

Tools

Figma

Problem

What Could Be Improved?

During a task, a driver may arrive at the assigned parking slip only to discover that another trailer is already there. The current workflow does not provide a way to reconcile the physical yard state with the system state while ppreserving the driver's original task. This creates a mismatch between what the system expects and what the driver can actually do. I took on the responsibility of figuring out how we might allow the driver to correct the yard state and continue the move with minimal interruption.

I began by mapping the existing workflow and identifying the point where the system diverges from the driver's real world experience.
